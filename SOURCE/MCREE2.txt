Chapter 6

Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine (MCREE)

================================================================================
6.1 Introduction
================================================================================

The BorderShield Dynamic Multi-Criteria Routing Algorithm (BDMRA) relies on the
Standard Dijkstra Shortest Path Algorithm to compute the optimal route between
the selected source and destination. However, before the routing process can
begin, every road segment within the operational road network must be assigned
an appropriate edge weight.

Traditional routing systems generally use physical distance or travel time as
the edge weight. Although this approach is suitable for conventional navigation
applications, it does not accurately represent the operational suitability of a
road under dynamic environmental conditions.

To overcome this limitation, BorderShield AI introduces a Multi-Criteria Risk
Evaluation Engine (MCREE).

The purpose of this engine is to evaluate every road segment using multiple
operational datasets and convert the collected information into a single
Operational Cost.

This Operational Cost becomes the dynamic edge weight supplied to the Standard
Dijkstra Algorithm.

Therefore, the Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine acts as the intelligence
layer of the BorderShield AI routing framework.

================================================================================
6.2 Purpose of the Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine
================================================================================

The primary purpose of the Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine is to evaluate
the operational suitability of every road segment before route computation
begins.

Unlike conventional navigation systems that primarily consider distance or
travel time, the proposed engine evaluates several operational parameters that
directly influence mission safety and route reliability.

The calculated Operational Cost represents the overall difficulty and risk
associated with travelling through a particular road segment.

Consequently, the routing framework recommends routes that are operationally
safer instead of simply recommending the geographically shortest route.

The objectives of the engine include:

• Evaluate every road using multiple operational datasets.

• Consider environmental and operational conditions simultaneously.

• Generate a single Operational Cost for every road segment.

• Produce dynamic edge weights for graph construction.

• Support intelligent route recommendation.

• Allow future expansion through additional operational datasets.

================================================================================
6.3 Why Risk Evaluation Is Required
================================================================================

Traditional routing systems generally assume

Edge Weight = Distance

This assumption is appropriate when road conditions remain relatively stable.

However, operational environments are highly dynamic.

For example,

Road A

Distance : 10 km

Weather : Heavy Snow

Visibility : Poor

Terrain : Glacier

Road Status : Open

Road B

Distance : 15 km

Weather : Clear

Visibility : Good

Terrain : Asphalt

Road Status : Open

Although Road A is geographically shorter, travelling through it may be more
difficult and operationally risky than travelling through Road B.

Therefore, physical distance alone cannot represent the actual suitability of a
road.

To solve this limitation, BorderShield AI replaces physical distance with a
dynamically calculated Operational Cost obtained by evaluating multiple
operational parameters.

================================================================================
6.4 Multi-Criteria Evaluation Concept
================================================================================

The Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine follows the principle that the
operational suitability of a road depends on the combined influence of several
parameters rather than any individual parameter.

Instead of making routing decisions using only one factor, the engine evaluates
all available operational information before assigning an Operational Cost.

The engine therefore provides a comprehensive assessment of each road segment.

The generated Operational Cost reflects the overall operational suitability of
travelling through that road under the current conditions.

================================================================================
6.5 Datasets Used by the Risk Evaluation Engine
================================================================================

The Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine collects information from several
master datasets.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Geographic Master
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose

Stores geographical information for every operational location.

Information Used

• Node ID

• Coordinates

• Operational Zone

Contribution

Provides geographical identity and supports graph construction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Road Master
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose

Defines the road network.

Information Used

• Road ID

• Start Node

• End Node

• Distance

• Road Type

• Road Status

Contribution

Provides the structural connectivity of the operational road network.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Terrain Master
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose

Stores terrain-related characteristics.

Information Used

• Terrain Type

• Surface Type

• Gradient

• Elevation

Contribution

Determines terrain difficulty for every road segment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Weather Master
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose

Provides environmental information.

Information Used

• Temperature

• Snowfall

• Visibility

• Wind Speed

• Weather Condition

Contribution

Represents environmental conditions affecting operational movement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Mission Information
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose

Allows routing decisions to adapt according to mission requirements.

Information Used

• Mission Type

• Mission Priority

• Vehicle Type

• Cargo Category

• Emergency Level

Contribution

Ensures routing recommendations remain aligned with mission objectives.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Future Operational Updates
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose

Supports continuous modification of operational conditions.

Possible Information

• Temporary Road Closure

• Accident Reports

• Maintenance Activities

• Security Alerts

• Operational Restrictions

Contribution

Allows dynamic adaptation of the routing framework whenever new operational
information becomes available.

================================================================================
6.7 Working Principle of the Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine
================================================================================

The engine evaluates one road segment at a time.

The complete evaluation process consists of the following steps.

Step 1

Identify the road segment.

Example

Road R023

L05 → L06

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 2

Collect information from all associated datasets.

Example

Road Master

Distance = 12 km

Road Status = Open

Terrain Master

Terrain = Glacier

Surface = Ice

Weather Master

Snowfall = Heavy

Visibility = Poor

Mission Information

Mission Priority = High

Vehicle Type = Supply Vehicle

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 3

Evaluate every operational parameter.

Each parameter contributes to the overall operational suitability of the road.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 4

Generate a single Operational Cost.

Example

Road R023

↓

Operational Cost = 48

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 5

Assign the Operational Cost as the edge weight of the road.

The road is now ready for shortest-path computation.

================================================================================
6.8 Output of the Risk Evaluation Engine
================================================================================

For every road segment, the engine produces the following outputs.

• Operational Cost

• Dynamic Edge Weight

• Risk Category

• Updated Road Status

These outputs are transferred directly to the BorderShield Dynamic
Multi-Criteria Routing Algorithm (BDMRA).

================================================================================
6.9 Interaction with the BorderShield Dynamic Multi-Criteria Routing Algorithm
================================================================================

The Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine does not determine the route.

Its responsibility ends after generating the Operational Cost for every road
segment.

The Standard Dijkstra Algorithm subsequently receives the dynamically weighted
graph and computes the optimal operational route.

The complete interaction is illustrated below.

Road Information

        │

Terrain Information

        │

Weather Information

        │

Mission Information

        │

Operational Updates

        │

        ▼

Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine

        │

        ▼

Operational Cost Generation

        │

        ▼

Dynamic Edge Weight Assignment

        │

        ▼

BorderShield Dynamic Multi-Criteria Routing Algorithm

        │

        ▼

Optimal Route Recommendation

================================================================================
6.10 Advantages of the Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine
================================================================================

The proposed engine provides several important advantages.

• Considers multiple operational parameters simultaneously.

• Produces realistic Operational Costs.

• Supports dynamic routing.

• Automatically adapts to changing operational conditions.

• Modular architecture.

• Independent of the routing algorithm.

• Easy integration of future datasets.

• Improves operational safety.

• Enhances intelligent decision-making.

================================================================================
6.11 Conclusion
================================================================================

The Multi-Criteria Risk Evaluation Engine represents the intelligence layer of
BorderShield AI.

Instead of modifying the Standard Dijkstra Algorithm, the proposed framework
improves routing quality by generating dynamic Operational Costs from multiple
operational datasets.

This separation between operational decision-making and route computation
creates a modular, scalable, and efficient routing framework capable of
adapting to continuously changing operational environments.

The generated Operational Costs form the foundation upon which the
BorderShield Dynamic Multi-Criteria Routing Algorithm computes the safest and
most operationally suitable route between the selected source and destination.
